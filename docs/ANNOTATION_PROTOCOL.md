# Human coaching annotation protocol v0.1.0

## Purpose

Create advice that is genuinely tied to the visible 30-second sequence. Do not
copy advice from an online clip or ask the evaluated model to generate its own
reference answer.

## Reviewer procedure

1. Watch the complete 30-second sequence at normal speed, then replay freely.
2. Identify the phase and the team by visible shirt/position description only.
3. Record two or more concrete observations, including temporal order.
4. State one priority problem; if the view is insufficient, say so.
5. Explain why it matters in this sequence.
6. Give a concise coach message plus a representative practice design.
7. Supply observable success cues rather than generic motivation.
8. Record off-screen, occlusion, camera, and ball-visibility limitations.
9. Cite any coaching principle source used; do not copy substantial text.
10. Mark the item approved only after quality control.

## Reference sources

These federation resources may ground terminology and practice design:

- FIFA Training Centre, Core principles of play:
  https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/game/game-analysis/landing-page-core-principles.php
- The FA Boot Room, Spotlight on pressing:
  https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/spotlight-on-pressing
- The FA Boot Room, Out of possession:
  https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/england-dna/how-we-play/out-of-possession
- The FA Boot Room, The three parts of possession:
  https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/the-three-parts-of-possession
- The FA Boot Room, Using constraints in coaching:
  https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/how-to-use-constraints-in-your-coaching-session

These sources discuss principles such as scanning, coordinated pressure,
cover/balance, compactness, support, transitions, and representative practice.
They are background sources. They do not establish what occurred in a selected
SoccerNet clip.

## Quality control

An approved item must be observable, internally consistent, actionable,
appropriately uncertain, and free of hidden SoccerNet metadata. Ideally use two
independent qualified reviewers and adjudicate disagreements. If only one is
available, report that limitation and do not calculate inter-rater agreement.

Test references live under `annotations/private/test` and remain inaccessible
during development and validation.
