CASE: A-0009 — Champions League performance insights: Bellingham between the lines — clip 1

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Real Madrid attacking midfielder (Jude Bellingham)

COACHING INTERVENTION
Develop the body control and adaptability to receive a pass on either foot when the actual delivery differs from the anticipated side, while maintaining good positional awareness in the space between opposition lines.

VISIBLE EVIDENCE / HUMAN OBSERVATION
This clip is discussed by UEFA Technical Observer Jan Peder Jalland as an example of Bellingham adjusting his position to receive between the lines against Juventus. Jalland's stated commentary: Bellingham has found space and adjusts very well; his awareness is very good; he is probably expecting the ball to his left foot, but it comes to his right, yet he has the body control to go both ways depending on the pass. The wider article context frames this within Real Madrid manager Xabi Alonso's description of Bellingham as a 'complete' player whose work between the lines was integral to the win, and notes Bellingham received the ball between the lines more times (17) than any other player in the match.

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Per Jalland's own coaching reflections in the article (general coaching guidance illustrated by this clip): start with mannequins and passes played in between the lines, with a player moving to find a striker or winger in behind. Progress to combination plays and finishing. Add central defenders as mannequins for midfielders, then gradually introduce live defenders and midfielders, eventually allowing counter-attacking play. Vary pitch size and number of players to control repetition versus game-realism — smaller pitches with fewer players demand more awareness and technical ability with more repetitions; larger, fuller setups are closer to the real game but allow fewer reps.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Player adjusts body orientation (open stance, backing away, moving sideways, or standing still as needed) rather than only relying on running to find space.
- Player can receive and control the ball effectively regardless of which foot the pass actually arrives on.
- Player shows awareness of surrounding opposition shape before and while receiving the ball between the lines.
- Player is not caught flat-footed or forced into a poor first touch when the pass direction differs from expectation.

SOURCE ATTRIBUTION
Jan Peder Jalland — UEFA Technical Observer; coach of Norway's Under-21 men's national team
