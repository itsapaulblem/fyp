CASE: A-0004 — Argentina short corner vs Mexico, clip 4

COACHING INTERVENTION
Objective: Recognise and exploit the space left behind opponents who
over-commit numbers to defend the short corner, and adapt the release
(carry into the box vs. shoot from range) based on how much space and
time is actually available.

Rationale (from source text): Mexico send two players out to defend
the short corner, unlike the single defender Argentina faced from the
Netherlands in clips 1 and 2. Fernández recognises he can exploit the
space behind those two defenders. When he receives the ball at the
edge of the area, rather than shooting immediately from range, he
carries the ball forward into the penalty box before finishing into
the far corner of the net. The article notes more broadly that across
these examples, Argentina's players demonstrated great awareness,
understood exactly what they wanted to achieve, and could read how
opponents would respond to their positioning at attacking corners —
using decoy movements to disturb the defensive structure and create
space for Fernández, whose ability to strike the ball hard and
accurately with both feet makes him a threat from range.

REPRESENTATIVE PRACTICE
Small-sided corner-kick rehearsal where the defending side is coached
to send two players out to press the short corner (rather than one),
so the attacking group must recognise and react to the extra space
this creates behind them. The Fernández-role player receives on the
edge of the area and must read in the moment whether to shoot
first-time or carry into the box, rehearsing both options. Finish
practice with an emphasis on placement (far corner) once inside the
box, using both feet.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Defending side commits two (or more) players to the short corner,
  leaving clear space behind them.
- Receiving player recognises this space immediately upon receiving
  the ball on the edge of the area.
- Receiving player correctly chooses to carry into the box (rather
  than shoot early) when the extra space and time allow it.
- Finish is placed accurately into the far corner of the net rather
  than blazed straight at the keeper.
- Overall sequence shows the attacking group reading the defense's
  specific response and adapting execution accordingly, rather than
  running an identical, rigid routine regardless of the defensive
  setup.
