CASE: A-0016 — Cucurella 1v1 defending, clip 3

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Chelsea left-back (Marc Cucurella), pressing in the middle-to-attacking
third against Lamine Yamal.

COACHING INTERVENTION
Concise intervention tied to visible evidence: Develop the
anticipation to read an incoming pass early and correctly judge
closing distance, so as to press the receiver aggressively before he
can control, turn, or retain the ball.

Evidence (per UEFA Technical Observer Rui Faria, discussing this
clip): Cucurella anticipates a pass being played to Yamal, judges the
distance correctly, and steps out to close down before the ball
arrives. He presses Yamal aggressively upon/before reception, denying
him the time and space to receive and turn, or to otherwise keep the
ball. The outcome is winning a throw-in for Chelsea. Faria's direct
assessment: "He presses aggressively, not giving him the time and
space to receive and turn for a 1v1 situation or even to keep the
ball."

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Anticipation-and-press practice: set up scenarios where a passer plays
into a receiver under varying distances/angles, with the defender
starting slightly off the receiver and coached to read the pass's
flight and trajectory as the trigger to close down, rather than
waiting for the ball to arrive. Reward presses that arrive tight
enough to prevent the receiver from taking a controlling first touch
that allows a turn. Vary the pass speed/distance to sharpen the
defender's judgment of when to commit to the press versus hold
position. This connects to Rui Faria's general 1v1 defending
principles cited elsewhere in the article: body positioning and
balance; focus on ball and space; and identifying the opponent's
weaker side.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Defender reads the pass early, beginning to close distance before or
  as the ball is played, not after it arrives.
- Defender's press denies the receiver time to take a controlling
  touch and turn.
- Receiver is forced away from goal, into a poor touch, or
  dispossessed/forced out of play.
- Press does not concede space in behind, reflecting sound judgement
  of when to commit.
