CASE: A-0001 — Argentina short corner vs Netherlands, clip 1

COACHING INTERVENTION
Objective: Create and exploit a numbers-up situation on the edge of the
penalty area at a short corner, using a delayed back-post grouping as a
decoy and a late, disguised run to the edge as the release option.

Rationale (from source footage description): Three Argentina attackers
hold near the back post while the two centre-backs jog up slowly to
arrive late in that area, manufacturing a back-post threat. This draws
defensive attention away from the edge of the box, where only one Dutch
defender is left to mark Messi at the short corner. Fernández drifts
late from a deeper starting position into the vacated edge-of-box space,
timing his move to arrive only once opponents have committed. Messi
lays the ball off to the unmarked Fernández, who strikes with his left
foot; the shot is deflected over the bar.

REPRESENTATIVE PRACTICE
Small-sided corner-kick rehearsal (attackers vs. defenders) isolating
one flank of the box. A cluster of attackers (plus 1-2 "late joiners")
occupies the back post on a delay while one attacker sets up for a
short corner near the corner flag and a second attacker starts deep and
holds his run. Coach freezes/rewinds reps to check defensive shape is
drawn toward the back post before releasing the short-corner pass, then
rehearses the hand-off/lay-off to the edge-of-box runner and the
first-time strike.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Back-post decoy group visibly holds the attention of multiple
  defenders (including at least one centre-back) before the short
  corner is played.
- Edge-of-box defender is drawn toward the short-corner ball-carrier,
  leaving the edge open.
- Second attacker's run into the edge space is delayed until opponents
  have committed, not started early.
- Ball reaches the edge-of-box attacker with no immediate pressure,
  allowing a clean strike on goal.
