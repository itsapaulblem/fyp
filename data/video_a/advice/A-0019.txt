CASE: A-0019 — Regaining possession high up the pitch — clip 2 (Scotland pressing to regain)

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Scotland pressing unit

COACHING INTERVENTION
Organise a coordinated, numerically matched team press immediately after losing possession, so the ball can be regained quickly (within seconds) rather than conceding space for a counter-attack.

VISIBLE EVIDENCE / HUMAN OBSERVATION
This local segment isolates one of the Scotland pressing examples presented in the FIFA source. Five Scotland players press against five England players to win the ball back, demonstrating an organised collective reaction after losing possession. The example shows Scotland coordinating the press and regaining the ball within seven seconds rather than relying on an isolated individual action.

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Representative general practice for the coaching intervention illustrated by this example: design phase-of-play practices where possession is deliberately lost in a matched numerical situation (e.g. 5v5), requiring the team without the ball to press immediately and collectively rather than relying on the nearest player alone. Use a regain-time target (e.g. seven seconds, referenced elsewhere in the article as the tournament average) to build urgency, and coach communication/coordination between pressing players to close space as a unit.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Multiple players engage the press simultaneously rather than just the nearest defender.
- Press is organised and coordinated, closing down passing options as a unit.
- Ball is regained quickly (within several seconds) rather than the opposition being given time to escape the press.
- Team does not concede large gaps or get bypassed while pressing collectively.
