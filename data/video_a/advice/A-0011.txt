CASE: A-0011 — Güler contribution, clip 2

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Real Madrid attacking midfielder (Arda Güler), operating in central
midfield and the attacking half-space against Juventus.

COACHING INTERVENTION
Concise intervention tied to visible evidence: Develop the awareness
and technical execution needed to drop into space between the lines
and release an immediate first-time pass to a teammate, maintaining
tempo and disguise in combination play.

Evidence (per UEFA Technical Observer Jan Peder Jalland, discussing
this clip): Güler changes depth, dropping into space to become
available to receive, showing awareness of the position of Brahim
Díaz and the surrounding play before receiving. He then receives the
ball and, without taking a controlling touch, plays a first-time
flick that connects directly to Brahim Díaz, continuing the attacking
move. Jalland's direct assessment: "he exhibits his excellent movement
and awareness by dropping down and producing the first-time flick to
Brahim Díaz." Wider match context notes Güler ranked second among Real
Madrid players for both key passes (six) and passes into the final
third (nine), and was the second-most frequent Madrid player (after
Bellingham) to receive the ball between the lines (12 times) — though
these are match-wide figures rather than specific to this clip.

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Per Jalland's general coaching reflections in the article (not written
specifically for this clip): begin with mannequins and passes played
between the lines, with a player moving to find a striker or winger in
behind, then progress to combination play and finishing. Add central
defenders as mannequins for midfielders, then gradually introduce live
opposition. For this particular skill, emphasise reps where the
receiving player must decide in the moment whether to take a touch or
play first-time, rewarding correct first-time execution when
tempo/space demands it. Vary pitch size and player numbers to adjust
the difficulty and time pressure involved in that decision.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Player recognises the right moment to drop and create a passing
  option between the lines.
- Player scans and is aware of a teammate's position before the ball
  arrives.
- Player executes a clean first-time pass/flick without needing a
  controlling touch.
- The pass finds the intended teammate accurately, sustaining the
  tempo of the attacking move.

REVIEWER PROVENANCE
The coaching commentary above is directly attributed to Jan Peder Jalland
(UEFA Technical Observer; coach of Norway's Under-21 men's national
team), a named, credentialed source quoted in the article discussing
this specific clip.
