CASE: A-0002 — Argentina short corner vs Netherlands, clip 2

COACHING INTERVENTION
Objective: Use two coordinated decoy actions in sequence — a receiving
run to draw one marker, followed by a run into the box to draw the
edge-of-area defender — to leave a shooter completely free on the edge
of the box.

Rationale (from source footage description): Unlike clip 1, Argentina's
players start from different positions here: both centre-backs are
already in the penalty area rather than arriving late, and a player is
stationed on the ball-side corner of the box, becoming the most
immediate threat to the sole Dutch defender guarding the edge of the
area. Fernández again hangs back to appear like a non-threat. Messi
starts from inside the box and runs to receive the ball, dragging his
marker with him. As soon as Messi receives it, the corner-stationed
player makes a decoy run into the box, dragging the edge-of-area
defender with him. This leaves Fernández completely free to shoot from
the edge of the area; the shot is denied only by the width of the post.

REPRESENTATIVE PRACTICE
Small-sided corner-kick rehearsal with fixed starting positions: both
defending-side centre-backs already inside the box, one attacker
stationed on the ball-side corner of the box, one attacker (Messi role)
starting inside the box, and a shooter (Fernández role) starting deep.
Sequence the reps so the corner-stationed attacker occupies the edge
defender first, then the inside attacker checks to the ball to drag his
own marker, then immediately breaks in behind to draw the edge defender
fully inside, releasing the shooter. Rehearse timing so the shooter's
run into space happens only after both decoys have committed.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Edge-of-box defender is initially occupied by the corner-stationed
  attacker before the ball is played.
- Receiver's run to the ball fully drags his marker away from the edge
  zone.
- Decoy run into the box happens immediately after the ball is
  received, pulling the edge-of-area defender inside with it.
- Shooter is left with no defender within closing distance when the
  ball arrives on the edge of the area.
- Shot is struck cleanly and on target (unlike this clip, where it
  strikes the post).
