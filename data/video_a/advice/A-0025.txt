CASE: A-0025 — Goalkeeper considerations when defending corners, clip 4 (Japan, Alexandre Pisano)

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Japan goalkeeper (Alexandre Pisano), defending a corner within a
seven-player zonal marking system.

COACHING INTERVENTION
Concise intervention tied to visible evidence: Develop a goalkeeper's
ability to exploit the space and clear sightlines provided by a zonal
defensive setup to delay their jump as long as possible, maximising
height and reach when intervening on an inswinging delivery.

Evidence (per Pascal Zuberbühler, FIFA's senior football and
goalkeeping expert, discussing this clip): This clip shows Japan
defending with seven players in their zonal setup. The goalkeeper,
Alexandre Pisano, has a clear view of the inswinging delivery and has
space to work in. His patience and timing are crucial, as his delayed
jump ensures he generates the maximum height needed to punch the ball
away. The article notes that, tournament-wide, first contacts made by
goalkeepers in zonal setups rose by 2% compared to the FIFA U-20 World
Cup Argentina 2023, and that goalkeepers must still take responsibility
for defending this zone alongside their zonal players, particularly
when attacking players encroach on the space between zonal defenders.

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Per the article's general framing of zonal marking systems (not
written specifically for this clip): rehearse corner defending using a
large zonal setup (e.g. seven zonal defenders), giving the goalkeeper a
clear, unobstructed view and working space. Coach the goalkeeper
specifically on delaying the jump — resisting the instinct to commit
early — using repeated inswinging deliveries of varying pace and
trajectory so the goalkeeper learns to read the ball long enough to
time a maximal-height jump. Reinforce the shared responsibility
principle: goalkeepers must still actively defend the zone alongside
zonal players, especially reacting to attackers encroaching into gaps
between zonal markers.

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- Goalkeeper maintains a clear, unobstructed view of the incoming
  delivery throughout.
- Goalkeeper does not commit to the jump prematurely.
- Jump timing is delayed just long enough to maximise height/reach at
  the point of contact.
- Goalkeeper successfully punches/clears the ball away from danger.
- Goalkeeper remains alert to any attacker encroaching into gaps
  between zonal defenders, not solely relying on the zonal structure.
