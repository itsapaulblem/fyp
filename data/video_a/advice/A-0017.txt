CASE: A-0017 — Atlético low-block defending, clip 1

TEAM / ROLE BEING COACHED
Atlético de Madrid defensive unit (back five), protecting central
areas of their own defensive third against Arsenal.

COACHING INTERVENTION
Concise intervention tied to visible evidence: Organise a compact low
block that prioritises closing central passing lanes (through the box
and in behind), deliberately conceding only wide, less dangerous
circulation passes around the outside of the defensive structure.

Evidence (per UEFA Technical Observer Edin Terzić, discussing this
clip): Atlético focused their low-block defending on protecting
central areas — the width of the penalty box — forcing Arsenal to
attack in the wide corridors instead. Terzić's direct assessment: "You
see Atlético in the central area with all their players. There's no
pass through, there's no pass in behind. They only allow the pass
around them." Atlético defended with a back five (as distinct from
Arsenal's back four discussed in later clips of the same article), and
the quality of this deep defending was underlined by Arsenal's shots
against the low block carrying a combined low xG (0.22 across five
attempts), none of which forced a save.

REPRESENTATIVE TRAINING PRACTICE
Small-sided possession/defending practice using a back five (or
matching the numbers being defended against): defending team is
instructed specifically to prioritise central compactness — no player
leaves a central passing lane open — while accepting that the
attacking side will have freedom to pass around the outside. Attacking
side is coached to attempt central through balls and balls in behind
as the "win" condition, while the defending side's success is measured
by denying these central options entirely, regardless of how much
possession/width play the attackers are allowed. Debrief on shot
quality/location conceded (emphasising low-value wide/deep attempts
over central, high-value chances).

OBSERVABLE SUCCESS CUES
- No central through balls or passes in behind reach an attacker in a
  dangerous position.
- The defending unit remains compact around the width of the penalty
  box rather than being pulled apart.
- Attacking side is forced to circulate the ball around the outside of
  the block rather than through the middle.
- Any shots conceded are low-quality (wide angle, distance, or low xG)
  rather than clear central chances.
- The defending shape holds its central discipline even as the ball
  moves from side to side.
