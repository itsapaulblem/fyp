P2 FIXED ATTENTION HINTS — FROZEN v1

Use the same three hints, in this order, for each declared development clip.
These are prewritten attention questions, not reviews of a model answer or
human observations of an event. Each turn asks for a complete revised
recognition with the original headings, frame citations, direct-observation/
inference separation, and no coaching.

HINT 1 — ANCHOR ACTION (sampled images 1–12)
Re-examine sampled images 1–12. What is the most specific opening action or
phase of play supported by the images? If it is a restart, identify its type
only when the ball's location, pitch markings, and player positions support
that identification. Give the best-supported interpretation, one plausible
alternative if relevant, and the frame evidence for or against each. If the
starting location or action is not visible, say that it cannot be determined
rather than making a confident guess. Correct your opening chronology.

HINT 2 — PASSES AND POSSESSION (sampled images 10–22)
Re-examine sampled images 10–22 in chronological order. Count only passes
whose before-and-after ball movement and participants are supported by the
sampled images; report the minimum clearly supported count, not a guessed
total for the unsampled intervals. For each possible change of possession,
identify the images and explain whether the ball, contact, and receiving team
are directly visible or inferred. Do not assign continuous possession merely
because one team's players are near the ball. Correct your chronology.

HINT 3 — FINAL ACTION AND OUTCOME (sampled images 20–30)
Re-examine sampled images 20–30 and compare them with your proposed event
sequence. What final action and immediate outcome can actually be identified?
Check the ball, any player contact, changes in movement, and any official's
visible behaviour. Is a scoring result, stoppage, ball out of play, continued
play, or none of these clearly supported? Separate an observed challenge or
player on the ground from a confirmed ruling; distinguish a visible result
from an assumed one. Identify contradictions in your earlier answer, state
what remains unknown, and give a complete revised recognition with calibrated
confidence.
