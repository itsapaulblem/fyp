EXAMPLE E-0016 — ALREADY APPROVED IN COMPARISON V1
Event: Corner kick.
In images 1-3, a red-and-white-striped player places the ball beside the
corner flag and corner arc. Images 4-10 show striped attackers and
white-shirted defenders gathering near goal. Images 11-12 show the delivery
reaching the crowded goal area. The goalkeeper in bright green appears to
collect the ball in images 13-15, after which the white team advances towards
midfield and the opposite penalty area in images 16-30.
Limits: The kick itself and individual contacts in the crowded box are not
clearly captured. Do not invent a successful header or goal.

EXAMPLE E-0017 — DRAFT; RESEARCHER APPROVAL REQUIRED
Event: Corner kick.
Image 10 shows a dark-shirted player approaching a stationary ball in the
corner arc beside the corner flag, where the goal line meets the touchline.
Images 4 and 9 show dark-shirted attackers and white-shirted defenders
positioned around the penalty area. Images 11-13 show players contesting
the delivery near goal, with a bright-green goalkeeper involved.
In images 15-16, the ball is away from the crowded goal area and players
move outward. Images 23 and 30 show subsequent play farther up the pitch,
including a white-shirted player with the ball in image 30.
Limits: The exact aerial contact and the player responsible for sending the
ball away are uncertain. Do not infer a goal from the delivery or goalkeeper
movement. The corner identification rests on the visible corner-arc setup,
not on a defensive wall or merely crowded player positions.
